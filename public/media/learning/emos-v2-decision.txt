6R decisions and the Enterprise Architect

Which modernization path fits this workload? The Enterprise Architect specialist compares the evidence across six canonical dispositions, through Ask EMOS and the governed assessment experience.

Retain means Govern and Monitor. Retire means Safely Decommission. Rehost means Migrate Infrastructure. Replatform means Transition Platform. Refactor means Transform Architecture. Repurchase means Replace and Adopt.

Review the recommendation, rationale, alternatives, risks, constraints, and the evidence that could change the decision. Target-platform claims must follow verified target-state evidence; EMOS should not invent a cloud choice.

New evidence can inform a follow-up assessment. Confidence does not equal readiness, and readiness does not equal approval. Next, resolve critical gaps, examine rejected alternatives, and bring the decision to an accountable human.
