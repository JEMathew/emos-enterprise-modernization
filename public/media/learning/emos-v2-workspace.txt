Where should you begin?  Open EMOS and choose your workspace context.  The Command Center summarizes the selected estate so you can identify workloads and evidence that need attention. Use Understand to explore the portfolio and Enterprise DNA.  Decide brings assessment and recommendations into focus.  Governance remains a required checkpoint before approved transformation. Transform organizes the execution plan. Keep the same estate and workload context as you move between these areas. Sign in for the private, owner-bound workspace.  Use the appearance control for light, dark, or system themes, and adapt the layout to your screen.  Next, select one workload and inspect its evidence.
