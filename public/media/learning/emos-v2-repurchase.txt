Repurchase — Replace & Adopt

When is buying a replacement the right path? Repurchase means Replace and Adopt when evidence supports moving to a commercial product.

The playbook structures approval, replacement selection, configuration, data and user migration, transition, retirement of the previous system, measurement, and learning.

EMOS tracks the plan; it does not purchase software or migrate users automatically.
