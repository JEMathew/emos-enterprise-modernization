Evidence gates and explicit human approval

Can AI approve its own recommendation? No. EMOS separates reasoning, deterministic readiness, and human authority so that a confident answer cannot authorize execution.

Readiness requires at least seventy percent evidence completeness and zero critical gaps. Needs Evidence blocks approval and execution progression. Ready does not mean Approved.

The governed workflow requires explicit human sign-off. An AI request to bypass approval cannot change these rules. Even complete evidence does not remove the human decision.

Inspect the evidence gaps, recommendation, alternatives, and risks before approval. Next, obtain accountable human review, then follow the disposition-specific plan. EMOS tracks governed work; it does not autonomously change production infrastructure.
