Replatform — Transition Platform

When is a platform change appropriate? Replatform means Transition Platform while keeping the decision grounded in workload and target-state evidence.

The playbook covers approval, target preparation, migration or transformation, validation, cutover, optimization, measurement, and learning.

EMOS does not provision a managed platform or execute a live migration.
