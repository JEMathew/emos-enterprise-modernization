What is EMOS V2?

What should your enterprise modernize, and what evidence supports that choice? EMOS is the Enterprise Modernization Operating System. It connects workload evidence, six R decisions, human governance, execution planning, and representative outcomes.

Follow Understand, Decide, Govern, Transform, and Outcomes. Begin with a sample estate or a sanitized portfolio. Inspect Enterprise DNA, identify missing facts, and compare the recommendation with alternatives before making a decision.

Ask EMOS is the conversational interface. A deterministic orchestrator routes reasoning to two Gemini-backed specialists: Portfolio Discovery and Enterprise Architect. Deterministic validation and governance enforce the rules. The orchestrator is not a third agent.

Evidence readiness never grants approval. A human must explicitly approve governed progression. Transform tracks disposition-specific workflows; it does not perform live cloud migrations. Measure and Learn show representative benchmarks and session-only notes. Next, choose an estate and inspect one workload.
