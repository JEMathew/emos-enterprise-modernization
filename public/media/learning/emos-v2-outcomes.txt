What can outcomes tell you about a modernization plan?  Measure presents Baseline, Target, and Observed values so you can discuss assumptions and differences in the chosen disposition's context. They are not readings from a live production telemetry, billing, or benefits-realization integration. Learn presents representative archetype lessons and lets you capture a session-only note.  Describe the assumption, observed difference, lesson, and possible relevance to future work. Session notes do not retrain the agents or automatically change recommendations.  Next, use these views to structure a review, then validate real outcomes in your approved systems.  Any future recommendation still requires current evidence and human authority.
