Portfolio Discovery and its specialist

What do we know about the estate, and what should we investigate next? Portfolio Discovery helps you examine sample or imported workloads in the context of their enterprise evidence.

The Portfolio Discovery specialist uses Gemini to reason about heterogeneous evidence, relationships, ambiguity, and missing facts. Ask EMOS provides access to this reasoning.

Structured output is validated before it reaches the workflow. Missing evidence remains a gap; a plausible explanation is not a verified fact. This is not an automatic connection to your live enterprise systems.

Review dependencies and prioritized investigations, then inspect Enterprise DNA for the selected workload. Next, verify the highest-value missing facts before moving into a six R decision.
