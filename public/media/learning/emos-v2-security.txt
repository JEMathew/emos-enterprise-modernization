Trust, guardrails, and evaluation

What keeps an AI answer from becoming an unsafe action? EMOS places deterministic controls around model reasoning, including input fencing, output validation, canonical six R checks, and secret redaction.

Adversarial requests to bypass governance are intercepted. Evidence requirements and explicit human approval remain authoritative, regardless of the wording or confidence of a model response.

Software tests, deterministic evaluations, and recorded live checks provide bounded evidence about tested behavior. They do not prove perfect AI, zero hallucinations, or universal safety.

When AI reasoning is unavailable, the interface discloses a deterministic fallback. Next, inspect the Evaluation Evidence and the workload's actual evidence gaps before relying on a recommendation.
