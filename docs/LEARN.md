## Implemented boundaries

Ask EMOS is the conversational interface to two Gemini-backed specialists: Portfolio Discovery and Enterprise Architect. The orchestrator is deterministic, not a third agent. Readiness (≥70% completeness and zero critical gaps) never replaces explicit human approval.

Transform plans and tracks governed work; EMOS does not perform live cloud migrations or mutate production infrastructure. Measure uses representative synthetic benchmarks. Learn includes representative archetype lessons and session-only notes, with no automated retraining.

## Media verification

The final MP4s and posters are served from the existing application under `/media/learning/`. Captions are rendered into the video pixels; transcript files are supplementary. Source inventory, generation receipts, and per-master QA records are in `docs/learning/v2/`.
